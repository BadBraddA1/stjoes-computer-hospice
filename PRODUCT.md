# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors who land on a joke / meme site about end-of-life care for computers. They're here for a laugh, not a service. Context is casual browsing, sharing with friends, or following a QR from a fake biz card.

## Product Purpose

ST Joe's Computer Hospice is a parody hospice for dying machines. Success is delight: someone smiles, shares the bit, and remembers "sorry your time is up." Not a real hospital; definitely a real bit.

## Brand Personality

Clinical sarcasm · Warm hospice parody · Deadpan tech humor

Voice is hospital-form meets IT support grief counseling — solemn labels, ridiculous patients, gentle cruelty toward beige towers.

## Anti-references

- Real medical / hospice marketing (earnest stock photos, soft pastels, "we're here for you" sincerity without the joke)
- Generic SaaS landing pages (feature grids, purple gradients, Inter)
- Dark cyberpunk "hacker" aesthetics for a computer joke

## Design Principles

1. **Commit to the bit** — every page should feel like a ward, form, or chaplain's note, never a normal product site.
2. **Clinical labels, ridiculous content** — mono kickers and solemn structure carrying absurd copy.
3. **One joke, fully dressed** — fewer sections done well beat a dashboard of gags.
4. **Identity over novelty** — navy + medical green + Fraunces / IBM Plex stay the house look.
5. **Playful, not mean** — roast the machines and the premise; keep people smiling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Interactive bits (quizzes, media) must work with keyboard and clear focus states. Decorative imagery gets empty or witty alt; meaningful portraits get real descriptions.
